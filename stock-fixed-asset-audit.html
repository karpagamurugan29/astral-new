Service Details

Stock & Fixed Asset Audit

Enhancing Asset Integrity Through Independent Verification.

Inventory and fixed assets represent significant investments for most organizations. Inaccurate inventory records, unverified assets, obsolete stock, and weak asset controls can directly impact profitability, financial reporting, and operational efficiency.

Astral Consulting Limited provides comprehensive Stock & Fixed Asset Audit Services designed to help organizations establish accurate inventory records, verify physical assets, strengthen asset management processes, and improve financial reliability.

Our systematic verification methodologies help management identify shortages, excesses, obsolete inventory, unrecorded assets, and control deficiencies while ensuring that accounting records accurately reflect physical assets.

Our Stock & Fixed Asset Audit Services Physical Inventory Verification, Inventory Audit, Warehouse Stock Verification, Fixed Asset Verification, Asset Tagging & Barcode Labeling, Fixed Asset Register Preparation & Reconciliation, Asset Identification & Coding, Capital Asset Verification Helping organizations improve inventory accuracy, strengthen asset controls, and enhance financial reliability.

Physical Inventory Verification

Independent Verification of Inventory Accuracy

We conduct comprehensive physical verification of inventories to confirm the existence, condition, quantity, and valuation of stock. Our verification process identifies shortages, excesses, obsolete inventory, and reconciliation differences, helping organizations improve inventory accuracy and strengthen inventory controls.

Inventory Audit

Enhancing Inventory Management and Control

Our Inventory Audit evaluates inventory management practices, stock movement controls, storage procedures, inventory valuation methods, and overall inventory governance. We identify operational inefficiencies and recommend improvements that enhance inventory accuracy, reduce carrying costs, and improve working capital management.

Warehouse Stock Verification

Strengthening Warehouse Governance and Inventory Integrity

We independently verify inventory maintained at warehouses, distribution centers, and storage locations to assess stock accuracy, storage practices, inventory movement controls, and physical safeguards. Our reviews help organizations improve warehouse efficiency while minimizing inventory losses and operational risks.

Fixed Asset Verification

Protecting Capital Investments Through Independent Verification

Our Fixed Asset Verification services confirm the physical existence, location, utilization, and condition of fixed assets. We reconcile physical assets with accounting records, identify unrecorded or obsolete assets, and strengthen asset management practices to improve financial reporting accuracy.

Asset Tagging & Barcode Labeling

Building an Effective Asset Identification System

We assist organizations in implementing structured asset identification through barcode labeling, QR coding, RFID tagging, and systematic asset numbering. Proper asset tagging enhances traceability, simplifies physical verification, improves asset tracking, and strengthens asset lifecycle management.

Fixed Asset Register Preparation & Reconciliation

Creating Reliable Asset Records

Our consultants develop comprehensive Fixed Asset Registers (FAR) by capturing complete asset information, unique identification numbers, locations, acquisition details, depreciation data, and ownership records. We also reconcile physical assets with accounting records to improve financial reporting accuracy and regulatory compliance.

Asset Identification & Coding

Standardized Asset Classification for Better Control

We establish structured asset coding methodologies that enable consistent asset identification, classification, tracking, and reporting across multiple locations and business units. Standardized asset coding improves operational efficiency, simplifies audits, and enhances asset governance.

Capital Asset Verification

Independent Assurance Over Capital Investments

Our Capital Asset Verification services validate major capital expenditures, project assets, and infrastructure investments by confirming physical existence, capitalization accuracy, utilization status, and accounting treatment. This helps organizations safeguard capital investments, improve financial reporting reliability, and strengthen capital asset governance.
