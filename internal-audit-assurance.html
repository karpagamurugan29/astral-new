Internal Audit & Assurance

Strengthening Governance. Managing Risks. Driving Business Performance.

An effective Internal Audit function is more than a compliance requirement—it is a strategic management tool that helps organizations strengthen governance, improve operational efficiency, manage risks, and achieve sustainable business growth.

Astral Consulting Limited delivers independent, risk-based Internal Audit and Assurance services tailored to the size, complexity, and industry of each organization. Our approach goes beyond identifying observations—we focus on root cause analysis, practical recommendations, and measurable business improvements that support management in achieving its strategic objectives.

With over three decades of experience across manufacturing, engineering, textiles, healthcare, hospitality, food processing, mining, automobile dealerships, plantations, and service industries, our Director-led team brings deep industry insight and practical business knowledge to every engagement.

Our Internal Audit & Assurance Services Internal Audit, Risk-Based Internal Audit, Compliance Audit, Operational Audit, Financial Audit, Internal Control Review, Internal Financial Controls over Financial Reporting (IFC), Forensic Audit & Investigation Helping organizations strengthen governance, improve controls, reduce risks, and create long-term business value.

Internal Audit

Independent Assurance to Strengthen Governance and Business Performance

Our Internal Audit services provide management and the Board with independent assurance on the effectiveness of governance, risk management, and internal control systems. We evaluate business processes, identify operational and financial risks, and recommend practical improvements that enhance efficiency, strengthen controls, and support informed decision-making. Our audits are tailored to your industry, business objectives, and organizational structure, ensuring meaningful insights that create long-term value.

Risk-Based Internal Audit

Focusing Audit Efforts Where Business Risks Matter Most

Our Risk-Based Internal Audit approach prioritizes high-risk business processes and emerging risks that could significantly impact organizational performance. By aligning audit plans with enterprise risk assessments, we help management proactively identify vulnerabilities, strengthen risk mitigation strategies, and improve governance. This approach ensures audit resources are focused on areas that deliver maximum business value.

Compliance Audit

Ensuring Regulatory Compliance and Corporate Accountability

Our Compliance Audit services evaluate adherence to applicable laws, regulations, internal policies, contractual obligations, and statutory requirements. We help organizations identify compliance gaps, reduce regulatory risks, strengthen governance frameworks, and promote a culture of accountability while minimizing potential legal and financial exposures.

Operational Audit

Improving Efficiency Through Process Excellence

Operational Audits assess the effectiveness, efficiency, and economy of business operations. Our consultants analyze workflows, identify operational bottlenecks, evaluate resource utilization, and recommend process improvements that enhance productivity, reduce costs, and improve overall business performance.

Financial Audit

Strengthening Financial Controls and Reporting Integrity

Our Financial Audit services evaluate the reliability of financial processes, accounting controls, and financial reporting systems. We review critical financial transactions, assess financial governance practices, and identify opportunities to improve accuracy, transparency, and financial discipline, enabling management to make confident business decisions.

Internal Control Review

Building Strong Internal Control Frameworks

A robust internal control system is essential for safeguarding assets and ensuring reliable business operations. Our Internal Control Review evaluates the adequacy and effectiveness of existing control mechanisms across key business functions. We identify control gaps, assess process risks, and recommend practical improvements that enhance governance, accountability, and operational resilience.

Internal Financial Controls over Financial Reporting (IFC)

Enhancing Confidence in Financial Reporting

We assist organizations in designing, evaluating, and strengthening Internal Financial Controls over Financial Reporting (IFC) in line with applicable regulatory requirements and recognized control frameworks. Our reviews help management ensure the accuracy, completeness, and reliability of financial information while strengthening financial governance and reducing reporting risks.

Forensic Audit & Investigation

Detecting Fraud. Protecting Business Integrity.

Our Forensic Audit and Investigation services assist organizations in identifying financial irregularities, investigating suspected frauds, evaluating control failures, and gathering evidence for management and legal proceedings where required. Through a systematic and confidential approach, we help organizations strengthen fraud prevention mechanisms, improve internal controls, and protect stakeholder interests.
